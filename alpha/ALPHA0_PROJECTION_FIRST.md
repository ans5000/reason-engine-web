# Alpha 0 Projection-First Flow

1. Preserve free reflection.
2. Generate an internal, explicitly provisional projection.
3. Derive at most three clarification questions from uncertainties in that projection.
4. Apply the answers to an updated projection.
5. Let the participant edit, remove, add and exclude material.
6. Generate the dossier from original text, projection, clarification answers and participant corrections.
7. Measure UC-001 outcome.

No projection is objective. A local rule-based projection must identify itself as such and must not be presented as model reasoning.
